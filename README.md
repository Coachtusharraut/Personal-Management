# Eagle Structure — LifeOS

**One mission:** grow through the Sahyadri campaign and keep life and business actions aligned with your goals.

## Live app
https://coachtusharraut.com/life/

Active WordPress plugin: **Eagle Structure LifeOS v1.4.3**. The page remains password-protected.

## Eagle Campaign
Bright portrait-first dashboard with full hero artwork, progress ring, XP, forts, task completion, Sahyadri map, animated fort captures and editable missions/subtasks.

## Life & Business Logbook
Capture daily plans, sales calls, meetings, health, learning, content, personal growth and business actions. Entries include notes, outcome, next action and follow-up date.

## Cross-device progress
v1.4.3 stores progress under the signed-in WordPress administrator account. Sign in with the same WordPress administrator account on every device. The page password is still required as a separate privacy lock. Existing per-browser records are merged once on first sign-in from each previous browser. See [v1.4.3 release notes](wordpress-plugin/v1.4.3/README.md).

## Closed-loop guidance
Closed loop is the coaching process: notice when actions drift from the goal, correct the next action and return to the goal. It is not a separate record category.

## AI status
Cross-device storage and structured capture are live. Automatic ChatGPT conversation ingestion, call transcription and a generative AI provider are not connected yet.
