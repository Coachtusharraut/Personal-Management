# Eagle Structure — LifeOS

**One mission:** grow through a gamified Sahyadri campaign and close the loop on daily life and business actions.

## Live app
https://coachtusharraut.com/life/

The active WordPress plugin is **Eagle Structure LifeOS v1.4.1**. The page remains published and password-protected.

## Home: Eagle Campaign
- Bright golden sunrise theme with an illustrated eagle and Sahyadri fort.
- Full-width responsive layout.
- Live progress, mission count, subtask count, XP and rank.
- Dynamic fort map, task/subtask checklist editing, capture animations and sounds.

## Menu: Life & Business
A separate logbook captures daily plans, business, sales calls, meetings, health, learning, content, personal growth, finance and other records. Each entry can track notes, outcome, next action, follow-up date and open/closed status.

## Personal mastery
v1.4.1 adds a one-time Emotional Mastery — Partner Conversations mission and a personal-growth reflection to the Logbook. The task practice covers emotional regulation, facts versus assumptions, offering help without seeking validation, and respectful boundaries.

See [v1.4.1 release notes](wordpress-plugin/v1.4.1/README.md).

## AI status
Structured capture and follow-up tracking are live; automatic chat ingestion, call transcription and a generative AI connection are not yet connected.
