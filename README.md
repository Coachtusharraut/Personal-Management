# Eagle Structure — LifeOS

**One mission:** grow through the Sahyadri campaign and let daily actions, feedback and course-correction keep life and business on track.

## Live app
https://coachtusharraut.com/life/

Active WordPress plugin: **Eagle Structure LifeOS v1.4.2**. The page remains password-protected.

## Campaign
Bright, golden Eagle dashboard; dynamic fort map; editable missions and subtasks; live progress, XP and fort-capture effects.

## Life & Business Logbook
Capture plans, calls, meetings, health, learning, content, personal growth and business actions. Keep entries short by default; eye icons reveal more detail. Use Active / Done for record status.

## Closed-loop guidance
Closed loop is the process—not a record category: notice when actions drift from the goal, correct the next action and get back on track. The app stores the records; guidance and coaching happen in the ongoing ChatGPT conversation.
