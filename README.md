# Eagle Structure — LifeOS

**One mission:** grow through the Sahyadri campaign and keep life and business actions aligned with your goals.

## Live app
https://coachtusharraut.com/life/

Active WordPress plugin: **Eagle Structure LifeOS v1.5.1**. The page remains protected by the single LifeOS password, shared across devices.

## One dashboard
- Eagle Sahyadri campaign and progress.
- Partner Network with editable downlines, Personal PV, recursive Group PV, PIN levels, percentage and people count.
- Recursive missions and nested subtasks with progress.
- Life & Business Logbook in the top-right menu.

## Task resources
Attach images, PDFs, audio, video and other files (10 MB per file) to any task depth. Supported media previews inside LifeOS; other file types download. YouTube watch, youtu.be, shorts, live and embed URLs play inside the expanded task.

## Partner structure and qualification
The zoomable/pannable partner visualiser reflects the same live hierarchy as the dashboard. Group PV determines the current Pin:
200→3%, 600→6%, 1,200→9%, 2,400→12%, 4,000→15%, 7,000→18%, 10,000→Silver 21%.
