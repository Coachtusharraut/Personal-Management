# Eagle Structure LifeOS — v1.4.0

**Live app:** https://coachtusharraut.com/life/  
**Plugin:** Eagle Structure LifeOS v1.4.0

## Visual refresh
- Warm ivory, bright gold and sunrise palette.
- Full-width app container with responsive desktop two-column and mobile single-column layouts.
- Illustrated eagle flying over the Sahyadri, sunrise, hills and fort.
- Larger, clearer progress numbers, headings and task labels.
- Removed the Level 01 / Sahyadri Rising label. The app no longer implies that another level is already present.

## Campaign
- The task dashboard remains the home screen.
- Every task has a map marker; fort map and progress recalculate from the task/subtask data.
- Click a fort marker to open its task.
- Task and subtask add/edit/remove and completion remain available.
- Fort capture effects include eagle flight, spark animations, optional chime and vibration.

## Life & Business Logbook
Use the top-right menu to open a separate records area.

Record categories: Daily Plan, Business, Sales Call, Meeting, Health & Energy, Learning, Content & Audience, Personal Growth, Finance & Admin and Other.

Each entry can store what happened, date, outcome, next action, follow-up date and open/closed status. Entries can be edited, removed, reopened or marked complete. The overview surfaces open loops, due/overdue follow-ups and closed items.

## AI status
This release adds structured record capture and follow-up tracking, not a connected generative AI provider. Chat-based automatic categorisation, call transcription ingestion, reminders and AI analysis remain future integrations.

## Migration and storage
The app retains the previous task list and completion marks, and adds an empty journal until entries are created. Records are stored in WordPress under the private browser-session identity. Different browsers/devices do not automatically share the same session. The /life/ page remains password-protected.
